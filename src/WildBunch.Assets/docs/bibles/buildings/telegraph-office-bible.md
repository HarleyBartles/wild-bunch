# Telegraph Office Bible

This document defines the identity rules for the telegraph office family.

## Identity contract

- The telegraph office is communications-first and administrative.
- Keep the frontage compact and practical.
- Keep notices, service cues, and utility language available.
- Keep the family distinct from the general store, sheriff office, and saloon.
- It should read as the town's utility and message hub.

## Family cues

- Emphasize communications and administration rather than commerce or social
  entertainment.
- Keep the read small, useful, and town-appropriate.
- Keep the architecture compact enough to stay legible at town scale.
- Telegraph poles, wires, insulators, posted notices, and service hardware are
  valid cues when they do not overwhelm the building.

## Prosperity notes

- Use `prosperity-bible.md` for the shared tier ladder.
- The telegraph office may vary utility hardware, notice clutter, trim, and
  facade finish across tiers.
- Do not change the family identity when the tier changes.

## Prompt-ready guardrails

- Do: Keep the communications and administration read with compact practical
  frontage, posted notices, and service-oriented cues.
- Do not: Do not turn the telegraph office into a store or saloon.

- Do: Vary finish and utility detail by tier instead of changing the building
  identity.
- Do not: Do not over-ornament it into a boomtown showpiece that loses its
  practical identity.
