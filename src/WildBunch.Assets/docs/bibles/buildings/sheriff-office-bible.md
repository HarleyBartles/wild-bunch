# Sheriff Office Bible

This document defines the identity rules for the sheriff office family.

## Identity contract

- The sheriff office is authority-first.
- Keep the official read restrained and practical.
- Keep badge or star cues available when needed.
- Keep the family distinct from the general store, saloon, and telegraph
  office.
- It should read as the town's law office, not a commercial frontage.

## Family cues

- Emphasize law-enforcement function over retail or entertainment.
- Keep the facade plain enough to feel official.
- Keep the architecture compact and grounded rather than ceremonial.
- A star, badge plaque, notice board, or jail-bar hint can carry the read if
  they stay small and controlled.

## Prosperity notes

- Use `prosperity-bible.md` for the shared tier ladder.
- The sheriff office may vary badge treatment, trim, porch rhythm, and facade
  finish across tiers.
- Do not change the family identity when the tier changes.

## Prompt-ready guardrails

- Do: Keep the authority-first read with a restrained facade, plain official
  windows, and any badge or jail-bar cues needed to keep it unmistakably
  law-enforcement rather than retail.
- Do not: Do not add storefront clutter, stacked goods, broad retail signage,
  or saloon-style social frontage.

- Do: Vary finish and authority markers by tier instead of changing the
  building identity.
- Do not: Do not let the sheriff office collapse into the general store
  silhouette or a generic frontier shack.
