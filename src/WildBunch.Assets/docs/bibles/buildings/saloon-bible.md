# Saloon Bible

This document defines the identity rules for the saloon family.

## Identity contract

- The saloon is hospitality-first and social.
- Keep public frontage, porch rhythm, and entertainment-facing silhouette in
  the family read.
- Keep the family distinct from the general store, sheriff office, and
  telegraph office.
- It should read as the town's most socially expressive building.

## Family cues

- Emphasize the social destination read.
- Keep the frontage active and inviting.
- Keep the architecture distinct enough to read as a saloon at town scale.
- Balconies, railings, ornamental false fronts, and stronger porch rhythm are
  valid cues when they stay consistent with the family silhouette.

## Prosperity notes

- Use `prosperity-bible.md` for the shared tier ladder.
- The saloon may vary ornament, porch finish, rail detail, and facade polish
  across tiers.
- Do not change the family identity when the tier changes.

## Prompt-ready guardrails

- Do: Keep the hospitality and social read with public frontage, porch rhythm,
  and a stronger entertainment-facing silhouette.
- Do not: Do not strip away the social frontage or turn the saloon into a
  store or office.

- Do: Vary polish by tier instead of changing the building identity.
- Do not: Do not over-formalize it into a civic building that loses the saloon
  read.
