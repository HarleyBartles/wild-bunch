# General Store Bible

This document defines the identity rules for the general store family.

## Identity contract

- The general store is commerce-first.
- Keep porch, awning, merchandise frontage, and display cues available.
- Keep the family distinct from sheriff office, saloon, and telegraph office.
- Keep the silhouette practical and reusable across the prosperity ladder.
- It should read as the town's supply point, not just a generic shop.

## Family cues

- Emphasize retail and goods more than authority or civic function.
- Keep the frontage readable as a place to buy supplies.
- Let the storefront gain polish through the prosperity bible instead of through
  a new architecture.
- Barrels, crates, porch goods, awnings, hanging signage, and a busy retail
  frontage are valid cues when they stay subordinate to the building shape.

## Prosperity notes

- Use `prosperity-bible.md` for the shared tier ladder.
- The general store may vary signage, trim, porch display, and facade finish
  across tiers.
- Do not change the family identity when the tier changes.

## Prompt-ready guardrails

- Do: Keep a commerce-first read with porch or awning, merchandise frontage,
  and clear goods cues.
- Do not: Do not let the general store drift into a sheriff office, saloon, or
  telegraph office read.

- Do: Vary polish by tier instead of changing the building identity.
- Do not: Do not replace the retail frontage language with official markers,
  entertainment cues, or communications hardware.
