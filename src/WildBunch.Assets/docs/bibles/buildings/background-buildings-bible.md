# Town Background Buildings Bible

This document defines the shared contract for the filler-building families.
These buildings support the town read without becoming the main feature.

## Supported families

- `background-house`
- `background-shop`

## Background building contract

- Use the shared building contract from `buildings-bible-master.md`.
- Keep both families on the same 5-view turnaround contract as the named
  buildings.
- Keep both families on the same four prosperity tiers as the named buildings.
- Keep the filler read visually secondary to the named building families.
- Keep the massing and silhouette small-town, practical, and unobtrusive.
- Keep the support families visually quieter than the canonical town-center
  buildings.

## Family-level intent

- `background-house`: supporting residential or domestic read, not a hero
  building.
- `background-shop`: supporting retail read, not a hero building.
- The house reads as a modest clapboard home with a porch and a plain roofline.
- The shop reads as a small local business with a simple storefront or porch
  display, but it stays quieter than the named general store.

## Prompt-ready guardrails

- Do: Keep filler buildings readable at town scale while staying secondary to
  the named town buildings.
- Do not: Do not let the filler buildings become the dominant town feature.

- Do: Keep the same five-view turnaround and four-tier ladder for both support
  families.
- Do not: Do not invent different camera rules, footprints, or tier counts for
  the filler families.
