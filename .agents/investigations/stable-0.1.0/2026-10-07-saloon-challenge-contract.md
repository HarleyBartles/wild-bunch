# Saloon identification, challenge and confrontation

**Status:** Working product contract from the user's supplied flow. Taking someone in is the current interaction to assess and correct; gunfight is a separate future feature. Exact resistance probabilities, conditions and penalties remain implementation/product design where not specified.

## Observation and declaration

Look around presents a person by distinguishing physical features. Observation alone does not name them, mark them guilty or authorize taking an unnamed suspicious person to the sheriff. The player chooses whether to challenge them. A challenge requires declaring a named identity, such as Ben Kilpatrick the notorious cattle rustler. A matching description in a clue is evidence the player can use, not a substitute for naming the person.

Identity declaration is an assertion, not confirmation by the game that the assertion is correct. Preserve the possibility of challenging a citizen or misidentifying a gang member. The server knows the actual encountered person and resolves consequences from that identity; the browser must not supply authoritative target identity or outcome. Declining to challenge is an ordinary player choice, not an instruction to manufacture a confrontation result.

## Current take-in capability

Attempting to take the declared person in is an intention, not guaranteed surrender. They may resist and escape, injuring the player. The user's example is a bottle blow, seeing the person's heels as they run, blacking out and losing five health points. Preserve the resistance/escape/health-loss capability; do not treat that example as specifying every resistance result, probability or universal damage amount.

**Citizen exception, explicitly settled:** A citizen always comes quietly in a take-in attempt because they have nothing to fear from identification by the sheriff. The sheriff releases the citizen and fines the player a few dollars for wasting time. Do not put citizens into the wanted-suspect resistance/escape roll or require a usable firearm to make them cooperate. No bounty or gang-roundup progress follows. The exact fine amount and insufficient-cash policy remain to be specified; this requirement establishes the outcome, not arbitrary numerical values. This applies to live take-in, distinct from the future gunfight rule where killing an innocent leads to arrest for murder.

**Gunpoint requirement, explicitly settled:** Taking a wanted person in requires owning a gun, not having ammunition. An unloaded gun can support the take-in attempt; it does not guarantee cooperation or remove the established resistance possibility. Without a gun, the wanted person refuses and the player cannot compel them. Refusal is not automatically the current code's Fled outcome: do not invent escape, injury or settlement from absence of a gun. Citizens still come quietly without a gun. Separate firearm ownership from loaded-firearm combat capability instead of using FirearmThreatAvailable, which currently requires ammunition, as a single predicate for both interactions.

Resolve lawful cooperation or resistance on the server, record the actual outcome and health/state changes through authoritative events, and replay those outcomes without rolling again. A failed capture must not pay a bounty or count toward gang roundup. Successful lawful settlement integrates with the separate gang-release and bounty contracts. Exactly when transport/settlement is represented, action/time cost, escape persistence and misidentifying an actual gang member remain to be reconciled with source and settled before implementation. Citizen release and a small wasting-time fine are explicitly approved product behavior; automatic firearm-based suspect surrender is not approved merely because that branch exists.

The inspected live path exposes declaration and Take to sheriff, then forces surrender for a usable firearm plus correct declaration, a fine for an armed wrong declaration, or escape otherwise. These are source behavior, not the complete product contract. Fix ordinary saloon presence reachability (DN-02) and distinguish intent from outcome while addressing the retained take-in path. The user confirms that the lack of a kill flow is unfinished scope, not accidental removal of a shipped interaction.

## Correct declaration and future sheriff recognition

**Current requirement, explicitly settled:** The player must correctly name the actual wanted person for a successful bounty settlement and gang-roundup credit. Capturing a different wanted gang member under the wrong name does not become a successful settlement merely because the sheriff could theoretically recognize them. Do not add automatic correction to the retained take-in path. The user approved the wrong-name live take-in aftermath: the sheriff releases the person, fines the player a small amount, and awards neither bounty nor gang-roundup credit. This matches the citizen settlement consequence while preserving the different confrontation behavior: citizens always cooperate; wanted suspects may resist. Exact fine values and insufficient-cash handling remain implementation design.

**Future feature:** Sheriff recognition of a misnamed wanted person depends on whether that person's poster has circulated to that town's sheriff office. This requires town-specific warrant/poster circulation and sheriff knowledge, distinct from the player's known posters; it cannot be implemented by checking only a globally generated warrant or the browser's selection list. Later design must specify recognition, correct settlement/payment and roundup effects, failed recognition, information timing, authoritative events/projections and tests. Record this as deferred recognition/circulation work, not a current exception to correct naming.

Current coverage must reject wrong-name bounty/roundup credit even when the actual person is another wanted gang member, while preserving correctly named settlement and the separate citizen consequence. Future recognition coverage must distinguish the same person's poster present versus absent at the receiving sheriff office and preserve those town-specific facts through replay.

## Future gunfight capability

The eventual player choice is take them in or challenge them to a gunfight. A gunfight must be won; Killed is a resolved outcome, not a result the client selects directly. The player must also have correctly named the person. Winning a fight does not establish a lawful bounty target. Taking a misidentified innocent person's body to the sheriff results in arrest for murder, such as killing the town doctor. Precise consequences for other wrong declarations must be specified rather than generalized from this example.

**Unequipped gunfight, explicitly settled:** The game allows the player to challenge a gunfight without a gun or without ammunition, and the player dies as a result. Do not disable or reject that challenge simply to prevent a losing choice. A gun and compatible ammunition are required to win, not prerequisites for submitting the challenge. With both, victory still depends on the future combat rules; ownership alone must not guarantee a win. Model player death as an authoritative outcome and preserve it through resume/replay. Keep this rule in the deferred gunfight work, rather than adding combat during take-in cleanup.

Future integration requires a playable combat-resolution contract, equipment/ammunition and weapon damage, legal challenge prerequisites, win/loss consequences, captured/body state, sheriff validation, truthful narration, event replay and terminal outcomes. Killing the actual culprit loses the hunt even if a corpse bounty can be collected; killing an innocent and being arrested is a separate failure rule. These consequences must not be conflated with successful settlement or implemented through a generic client-selected dead/alive flag. Gunfight addition is deferred and does not enlarge 0.1.0 cleanup into new combat development.

## Behavioral coverage and dependencies

Protect observation without identity confirmation, mandatory named declaration, actual-person validation without trusting browser identity, resistance with real health loss and escape, no payout/roundup for failed capture, lawful successful settlement and replay/resume of each result. Retire tests that protect guaranteed surrender if resistance is required in that context; keep meaningful identity/legality negatives. Future gunfight tests must distinguish winning from correct identification, lawful corpse settlement from innocent murder, and culprit-death loss from other gang settlements.

Citizen coverage must independently prove quiet cooperation, sheriff release, the wasting-time fine and absence of bounty/roundup progress, including when the player lacks a usable firearm. Preserve the same result and cash consequence through replay. Do not use citizen examples to assert that wanted suspects always cooperate or always receive the same mistaken-identity consequence.

Take-in coverage must distinguish gun ownership with zero ammunition from no gun: the former supports an attempt, the latter produces refusal without capture or bounty. Do not assert that owning ammunition without a gun is sufficient. Future gunfight coverage must prove that unequipped challenges remain reachable and cause death, rather than a disabled button or validation rejection; compatible loaded equipment permits combat resolution without guaranteeing victory.

This capability consumes public identities and player evidence from PG-005/008, uses equipment/health from inventory and resource mechanics, produces settlement for gang-roundup release, and feeds hunt terminal outcomes. Track the future gunfight separately from current take-in correction.
