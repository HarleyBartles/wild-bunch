# AI prose fatigue evidence notes

## Use and limits

This profile is a clean-room operational synthesis of the registered primary
studies below. The records support bounded reviews of clusters, regularity,
specificity, and voice preservation. They do not establish universal reader
fatigue, identify authorship in an individual draft, or license token bans.
Full bibliographic records are retained in
`../../../../assets/authority/CITATIONS.md`; no paper is vendored.

## Evidence records

- `sourati-2026-linguistic-diversity`: multi-domain empirical evidence of
  reduced writing-complexity variance across the reported datasets and models.
  It supports contextual review of homogenisation and local regularity, not a
  rule that variation is always better.
- `miletic-falk-2026-scientific-communication`: domain-specific evidence from
  NLP scholarly communication, including lexical, syntactic, and reading-pilot
  results. The small subjective pilot and scholarly genre limit generalisation.
- `agarwal-2025-cultural-homogenization`: a controlled India/US English-writing
  study supporting cautious preservation of supplied cultural and personal
  choices. It does not support culture or identity inference.
- `kobak-2025-excess-vocabulary`: corpus-level biomedical-abstract evidence of
  post-2022 vocabulary shifts. It cannot identify individual texts or make a
  word undesirable by itself.
- `weber-wulff-2023-detection-tools`: a dated, tool-specific detector audit.
  Its operational boundary is abstention from detector scores, authorship
  conclusions, and evasion advice.

## Bounded operational inferences

The eight pattern families are drafting hypotheses or contextual inferences
from those measurements. The registered studies do not isolate manufactured
conversation, throat-clearing, synthetic affect, semantic emptiness, or
repeated contrast as universal causal fatigue mechanisms. Those families
therefore use weak or emerging evidence classes, require visible clusters and
a concrete audience cost, and include preserve and abstention cases.

## Method

The profile separates measured findings from operational inference; treats
corpus prevalence, reader reaction, authorship, and quality as different
claims; and requires the smallest meaning-preserving repair. It is reviewed on
2026-08-25 and scheduled for evidence review after 2027-02-25.

## Counterarguments

Repetition, contrast, em dashes, triads, rhetorical questions, fragments,
directness, signposting, and regular structure are legitimate human devices.
They may aid rhythm, comparison, accessibility, legal precision, relationship,
or navigation. A pattern is actionable only when the declared context shows a
material cost and no higher-authority requirement protects it.
