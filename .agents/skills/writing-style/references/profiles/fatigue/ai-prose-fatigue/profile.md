# AI prose fatigue profile

Use this profile to review visible clusters of repeated, predictable, generic,
or low-information prose that may tire this draft's audience. It is not an AI
detector and supplies no universal preferred style.

## Decision sequence

1. Name the repeated or mismatched behavior and its local density.
2. Name the audience, genre, and concrete reading cost.
3. Check the pattern's evidence class, scope, preserve conditions,
   limitations, and review horizon in `patterns.json`.
4. Preserve factual distinctions, useful parallelism, deliberate rhetoric,
   accessibility, and authorised voice.
5. Offer the smallest repair that retains meaning and voice. If the evidence
   is stale or context is insufficient, return `candidate` or `abstain`.

## Active families

- Low-information affirmation: challenge clusters of unsupported emphasis,
  never an isolated word such as `real`.
- Synthetic profundity: challenge repeated contrast that adds no distinction;
  preserve contrast that carries the argument.
- Manufactured conversationality: challenge repeated audience cues without a
  reader job; preserve relationship, accessibility, and deliberate intimacy.
- Editorial throat-clearing: compress repeated prefaces only when they delay
  the claim or evidence.
- Predictable cadence: vary local shape only when regularity obscures hierarchy
  or exhausts attention; preserve useful parallel form.
- Synthetic affect: ground repeated positivity or emotion in specific evidence
  or consequence.
- Semantic emptiness: replace polished abstraction with actors, actions,
  evidence, or decisions when those facts are available.
- Voice flattening: restore authorised choices when generic rewriting has
  overridden the declared voice card.

Load `sources.md` only when inspecting evidence scope or refreshing the dated
profile. Full finding and golden contracts live in `../../../profile-contract.md`.

## Hard boundaries

Do not infer authorship, optimize against detectors, ban exact tokens, inject
defects, remove a deliberate device without reader-cost evidence, or strengthen
a claim while repairing style.
