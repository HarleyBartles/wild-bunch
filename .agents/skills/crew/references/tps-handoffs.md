# TPS handoffs

TPS is the Crew handoff cover-sheet discipline. It is not random compliance and not proof that the underlying work is true. It is a compact, falsifiable wrapper that shows what a role consumed, what it did, what it hands forward, and what the next role must not assume.

A role output that another role or skill must consume is TPS-noncompliant if it lacks the proof needed by the consumer. Return TPS-noncompliant output to the owner that must shape it.

## Base TPS cover sheet

A TPS cover sheet answers:

1. Input consumed: what task, source, terrain, route set, authority proof, or prior role output was received?
2. Domain work performed: what did this role inspect, classify, filter, select, reject, or preserve?
3. Output handed forward: what exact artifact, route set, lawful subset, plan, fallback posture, or receipt is being passed on?
4. Falsification notes: what can the next role test, what is unresolved, and what must not be assumed?

TPS can be one sentence when the handoff is simple. It becomes explicit when work feeds another role, a buster, a worker, a durable issue, a claimed GREEN, or a false-green investigation.

## Role-specific TPS expectations

Index TPS names starting terrain, provenance partitions, inspected landscape, unavailable sources, and candidate starts. It must not pretend unknown terrain is known.

Silk TPS names route and goal candidates with reachability, plausibility, trap/false-goal risk, and rejection reason. It must not certify legality.

Writ TPS names lawful, unlawful, and conditionally lawful routes with exact authority, actor, surface, scope, and artifact form. It must not select Plan A.

Klause TPS names the selected start, Goal A, Plan A, and why nonselected lawful candidates were rejected or deferred. If the candidate set is overwide because upstream shaping is missing, it returns the work to that owner with proof.

Rollback TPS names Plan A pressures, abort triggers, cleanup, recovery, Goal B conditions, and booby-prize exclusions. It must be attached to Klause's selected Plan A.

Receipt TPS names each prior role's accepted TPS, any missing or contradicted link, and the durable preservation route when lawful. It must not launder unresolved work into a settled record.

## No-op rule

A role may no-op only with a TPS reason proving its domain is not materially engaged. `Nothing for me` is not a valid handoff.

## Consumer rule

A consuming role should be able to falsify the producer's TPS without redoing the whole producer role. TPS is a framework for inspection, not a guarantee of correctness.
