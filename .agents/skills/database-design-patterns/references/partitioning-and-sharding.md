# Partitioning and sharding

Use this reference when planning partitioning or sharding. Partitioning
improves manageability and some query patterns; sharding adds distribution
complexity and should be a last resort after indexing and schema optimization.

This guidance is first-party synthesis supported by the BCcampus textbook
chapter on partitioning and sharding and the Red Book's distributed-database
material. Keep the guidance bounded to fundamentals and decision criteria, not
operational details of a specific platform.
