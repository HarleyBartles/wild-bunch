# Data modeling

Use this reference when designing or reviewing a data model. Start by
identifying whether the work is conceptual, logical, or physical, then choose
the modeling notation and level of detail that matches the audience.

The authority is the BCcampus *Database Design – 2nd Edition* chapters on the
design process and data models. Adapt the ER and relational model descriptions
into operational guidance. Do not reproduce full academic definitions; focus on
the decisions the modeler must make.
