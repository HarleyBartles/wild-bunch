# Stable 0.1.0 engineering baseline

**Status:** Approved by the user on 2026-10-07 for roadmap authoring and iterative bounded planning. The [cleanup roadmap](../roadmaps/2026-10-07-stable-0.1.0-cleanup.md) sequences this programme. Approval does not authorize implementation of its workstreams or resolve the explicitly listed product and compatibility decisions.

## Outcome

Produce a stable `0.1.0` development release that we can build on. Features included in that release work through their real player or developer entry points, preserve their invariants, survive supported persistence and replay, and have meaningful behavioral coverage. Half-built features that cannot meet that boundary are removed from the live release scope and recorded for later implementation. Feature completeness, a finished hunt, public hosting and an availability promise are not release requirements.

Stable means a truthful, maintainable engineering base with a deliberately limited product surface. It does not mean preserving every endpoint, enum, component, test or historical implementation. Nor does it mean accepting broken behavior merely because the game is alpha. We can ship a smaller incomplete game; we cannot describe an unreachable or snapshot-only shell as a working feature.

This specification governs the first workstream of the [cloud roadmap](../roadmaps/2026-10-06-cloud-playability.md). Account isolation, hosted developer isolation, containers, infrastructure and cloud promotion remain subsequent work under the [cloud specification](2026-10-06-cloud-playability-and-releases.md). A local `0.1.0` baseline cannot be deployed publicly until those access boundaries exist.

## Authority and artifact custody

The [investigation entrypoint](../investigations/stable-0.1.0/README.md) routes the source findings, test dispositions and working product records. They preserve what was discovered, including earlier proposals subsequently corrected by the user. They are evidence and design inputs, not execution instructions or proof that a fix works. Static findings must be checked against the live source when their plan is written; conditional risks must not be promoted to reproduced bugs.

This specification owns the programme's scope, invariants, workstream outcomes and release gates. The eventual cleanup roadmap owns dependencies and delivery order. Each accepted implementation plan owns a bounded source change and its validation. The authoritative feature matrix owns current product promises and directed dependencies. ADRs own durable decisions and their history. Doctrine, contracts, runbooks and playbooks own current engineering obligations. Unslop profiles own recognition and correction of recurring mistakes against those authorities.

Keep investigation material in `.agents/investigations/stable-0.1.0/`, specifications in `.agents/specs/`, roadmaps in `.agents/roadmaps/`, and actual execution plans in `.agents/plans/`. Do not interpret investigation filenames, findings or proposed dispositions as implementation approval. Preserve useful source evidence when promoting decisions; consolidate competing current authority rather than copying it into another independently maintained document.

The [release-foundation plan](../plans/2026-10-06-versioned-release-foundation.md) remains provisional and blocked on reconciliation with this specification and the accepted cleanup roadmap. Its implementation checklist is not the first cleanup plan. The retained unslop adoption, scoped AGENTS replacement and obsolete placement-test removal remain existing work; do not redo them or pretend the rest of the cleanup is already implemented.

## Disposition before remediation

Every finding and feature claim receives an explicit disposition before its affected implementation plan is accepted. Preserve the investigation IDs and link grouped findings to their owner; one underlying behavior can cover several findings without creating one ticket or test per finding.

| Disposition | Meaning and required treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Retain and repair | A release capability with a coherent promise. Repair its actual command/read/player path and tests; name supported failures, resume and replay. |
| Retire and defer | A partial or unnecessary capability excluded from 0.1.0. Remove its live producers, availability, consumers and misleading claims; preserve shared behavior, selected historical support and a separate future-addition story. |
| Consolidate | Competing owners, duplicate mechanisms or fragments of one behavior. Select one authority, move consumers and transfer useful coverage. |
| Historical support only | A retired producer still has supported persisted facts. Keep or migrate the necessary readers/effects without making the feature available again. |
| Correct guidance or history | A stale claim, implementation-heavy ADR or ineffective route. Repair current guidance and preserve truthful historical decisions and deviations. |
| Explicitly defer | A nonblocking concern with its consequence and owner recorded. A defect in an included feature's required invariant cannot be hidden in this category. |
| Investigate or decide | Evidence or a consequential decision is missing. Name the exact uncertainty and block only the dependent plan; do not invent product intent. |

No material audit finding may disappear through a file move or feature removal. The roadmap must account for the top-level F findings, ADR dispositions, test-quality dispositions, AP-01 through AP-20, PS-01 through PS-17, DN-01 through DN-16 and WB-01 through WB-24. Coverage means an accepted remedy, justified retirement, selected history treatment or explicit nonblocking deferral, not a checked box asserting the audit was read.

Each roadmap row links its finding groups and declares an observable exit. Before accepting its plan, identify which existing tests protect the wrong state, which useful coverage transfers, and what genuine behavioral gap remains. A source correction, its test correction and relevant current-truth updates form one deliverable; a later test-cleanup row cannot excuse shipping the newly corrected behavior without proof.

## Product boundary

A feature is an observable capability with a coherent lifecycle, not a folder, endpoint or type. Player features, developer capabilities and platform requirements remain distinct. The durable matrix will live at `docs/features.md`, with one authored authority and lifecycle routes requiring contributors and reviewers to read and update it when a product promise or dependency changes. Its initial publication is a reconciliation task, not copying the working inventory and labelling everything shipped.

Each matrix entry needs a stable ID, audience and promise, actual entry point, prerequisites, supported outcomes and failures, resume/history contract, implementation assessment, release disposition and directed dependencies. Dependencies state whether a capability requires another capability, consumes its output or changes its state, including conditional prerequisites. Record static assessment separately from runtime evidence. Retired features retain a removal record linked to a distinct future-addition record.

Layout editing must either have a lawful preparation entry point and observable effect or be deferred. Local developer tools can remain in the development baseline. Public/preprod admission and exclusion are subsequent cloud work, not a claim earned by hiding a menu here.

## Architecture and state invariants

Preserve the selected DDD, strict CQRS and event-sourcing posture. `GameSession` remains the live gameplay aggregate boundary. Internal children own cohesive state and rules, accept narrow context, and return outcomes/events without mutating siblings or owning persistence. Do not create child repositories, a generic NPC framework or a new event-store platform to perform cleanup.

Commands validate intent and establish facts through typed events. Event application reconstructs recorded facts without sampling randomness, using the current clock or consulting mutable content to replace established history. Application orchestrates use cases, fresh-state retries and safe mapping; gameplay legality belongs in Domain. API maps transport. Persistence appends/stores/reconstructs and owns envelopes and schema, not gameplay rules.

Queries use their supported read ports and player-safe models, without gameplay mutation, event creation or gameplay commits. Cache reconstruction is infrastructure work and cannot invent game facts. Command responses can legitimately return safe read DTOs after commit. Unknown setup values remain unknown; mappers, narration and UI must not fill them with fictional locations, health, provenance, ranking or successful outcomes.

Recorded generation, preparation, salt changes, inventory effects and phases must be reconstructible from the accepted history boundary. Retained developer preparation needs replay-complete genesis; zero-event snapshot-only preparation cannot remain the creation route for new event-authoritative sessions. Existing exceptional data is handled explicitly under historical policy.

Retries reload authoritative state and repeat decisions against that state; neither a fake returning the same mutated object nor generation outside the retry proves safe concurrency. Illegal or stale intent is rejected according to the actual contract. A lawful unsuccessful action may consume time/resources and emit events; do not impose a universal failure-means-no-change rule.

Snapshots, component rows and projections are reconstructible caches for event-backed sessions. Version equality, JSON validity or a nonempty row set does not prove validity, completeness, freshness or a coherent stream position. Correct reads and command loads must converge from intact history when supported caches are stale, missing, partial or malformed. Unrecoverable history fails explicitly instead of creating replacement entropy or defaults. Handle known database append constraints specifically; unrelated uniqueness failures must not masquerade as concurrency conflicts.

## History and migration boundary

Before removing event types, changing generation meaning or altering preparation/replay contracts, settle which existing pre-alpha histories remain supported. Choices can include versioned legacy interpretation, forward migration preserving recorded facts, or explicit invalidation of named unsupported playthrough classes. The user's acceptance of disposable playthroughs permits explicit loss; it does not select a universal reset or excuse a missing migration design.

Preserve applied schema migration history and use forward migrations. Maintain registered upcasters and the canonical loader for supported payloads. Do not fabricate missing historical ownership, salt, captured identity or generation facts. Retirement can stop new event production while retaining compatibility-only types and effects. Old unrelated-criminal payments must either retain their historical effects through the selected reader/migration path or belong to an explicitly invalidated playthrough class.

Record actual schema/event/data removals, including accidental losses and unknown reasons. Do not rewrite ADRs so past drift looks intentional. Truthful decisions remain historical truth: use successors, partial supersession or dated notes. Editorial consolidation around durable decisions may remove implementation narration only while preserving the original decision and material history. Future feature promises belong in the backlog/matrix rather than masquerading as current ADR truth.

Use the existing unslop observation loop to preserve recognition cues and corrective behavior from this cleanup. Promote reusable guards where the evidence supports them, with scope and false-positive boundaries; route them from the lifecycle/topical surfaces agents actually enter. Distinguish missing routing, ignored guidance and an ineffective correction. Do not count several findings from this one audit as independent recurrence, or declare the guards effective merely because their links resolve.

## Test design and acceptance

The full test audit and layer follow-ups define the existing owners to strengthen, consolidate or retire. They are not a requirement for one regression per finding. Correct assertions that protect bad behavior alongside the source correction. Retire feature-only assertions alongside feature retirement, retaining shared and selected compatibility proof. Preserve useful small negative/value tests and independent Domain, HTTP, PostgreSQL and browser boundaries.

Each meaningful scenario states the legal precondition, real operation, independent expected outcome and broken behavior it would detect. Replay starts from original genesis/history, not an already-mutated current case. Equality is supplemented by an independently correct oracle because command and replay can agree on the same bug. Do not seed final presence to prove normal saloon reachability, heat another session to prove a reset, accept bribe payment as evidence of food theft, or use a renderer double that omits scale semantics to certify hover behavior.

| Proof family | Required behavior for retained scope |
| --- | --- |
| Lifecycle and replay | Real seed/name/options submission; same established prologue after resume; lawful first arrival without journey time; recorded preparation/salts; confirmed archive and uncertain-response recovery. Wrong-phase/invalid intent is handled without false progression. |
| Query and projection safety | Populated hidden source truth produces safe actual player serialization; repeated player/dev reads do not mutate gameplay or append events. Unknown states stay unknown and occurrence metadata remains recorded truth. |
| Persistence and concurrency | Production registration, independent contexts and fresh retry state; intact history recovers supported corrupt/missing/stale/partial caches; malformed/unsupported authoritative history fails explicitly. Append conflicts cannot pay or apply effects twice. |
| Resources and legal transitions | Purchase affordability/quantity/duplicate-equipment negatives; horse removal with saddle retention; canteen ownership/refill; travel distance and theft/depletion effects; completed journey cannot continue advancing. |
| Knowledge and take-in | Retained clue sources and lawful spotting actually execute; identity remains a player declaration; correct/wrong/citizen/refusal/resistance outcomes obey the accepted scope and persist. If retained, roundup counts distinct lawful settlements and remains independent of identity discovery. |
| Web resources and interactions | Shared readers observe changed authoritative facts; delayed game-A work cannot overwrite selected game B; command pending/error and legal unsuccessful outcomes are visible; preview matches current intent/state; real keyboard/focus, renderer geometry and narrow-width behavior are verified where changed. |
| Tooling and release | Real validator negatives, portable bus behavior, staged-tree isolation and hosted committed-tree parity; pinned lint policies; coherent version/artifact identity and declared release limits. Source-filename inventories are not substitutes. |

Run the smallest relevant focused lane during implementation, then the repository's required canonical gate for the actual intended tree. PostgreSQL and browser boundaries need their real runtime when their plan changes that behavior. No test or browser execution is claimed by this specification. Keep test output and generated evidence out of durable repo receipts; ship tests, contracts and decisions, with validation evidence in review/delivery surfaces.

## Decisions reserved for bounded brainstorming

The programme cannot silently choose old-history support/invalidation, which unresolved partial capabilities remain in the release, unsupported preparation/layout controls, or a finished hunt contract. Workstream A must identify those gates and settle the affected disposition before its plan is accepted. Other blocking choices include the retained release threshold, depletion behavior, fine/insufficient-cash policy, resistance tuning and single-store numerical price consolidation when those mechanisms are included. These are named design boundaries, not permission for a planner to invent rules.

Exact directory names below the chosen ownership boundaries, helper extraction, equivalent algorithms and test consolidation are planning choices. Tool selection and pinned style rules are settled in the tooling slice after checking the existing runtime. Historical intent, missing event facts and future gameplay cannot be inferred from test titles or framework conventions.

For each roadmap slice, brainstorm only its observable outcome, affected seams, prerequisites, history implications, test transfer and blocking decisions. Record newly discovered feature-shaped work in the matrix. If it exceeds repair of the selected retained contract, assign it to a future feature or explicitly change the release disposition with the user. Return to the current slice after recording it. Do not resume an endless feature-design interview to answer a cleanup dependency.

## Release exit

`0.1.0` is ready only when the included matrix entries have real reachable paths and meaningful proof, excluded scope cannot intrude on those paths, retained history follows the selected migration policy, and authoritative state remains event-backed with strict read/write separation. Material audit findings have dispositions and affected plans have passed their acceptance; remaining known limitations describe excluded or nonblocking scope honestly.

Guidance has one current owner per obligation; ADR discovery and lifecycle updates are routed; the command bus and lint/style policy are enforceable; normal local and hosted validation operate on the corresponding candidate state. The versioned release process identifies one immutable release source/artifact set. No claim of public safety or cloud playability follows until the separate ownership and environment work is delivered.

The next artifact is the cleanup roadmap derived from this reviewed specification. It must allocate every finding group, expose dependencies and decision gates, and contain only bounded outcome rows. Write and brainstorm implementation plans iteratively against the then-current source. Do not prewrite the entire programme's execution instructions or execute the provisional release plan as a shortcut.
