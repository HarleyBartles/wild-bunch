## Dependency rules

The table gives a default consecutive route, not a claim that all rows depend on every earlier row. Row 01 sets scope/history gates for retirement and behavioral work. Row 02 establishes current-truth and ADR maintenance obligations; affected rows then keep those owners current. Rows 03-04 provide enforcement early. Row 05 precedes restructuring of excluded code. Rows 06-08 supply authority/load/read contracts consumed by rows 09-12. Individual defects may require an earlier coordinated prerequisite; expose that dependency in the next plan rather than temporarily blessing the broken path.

Rows 09-12 include the relevant frontend interaction and tests when their backend contract changes. Rows 13-15 then consolidate surviving ownership and cross-surface behavior. They must not be used to delay correct pending/error handling or coverage essential to an earlier row's outcome. Row 16 addresses only residual source dispositions, and row 17 only residual test dispositions. Neither is a dumping ground for unprotected fixes. Row 18 requires all applicable exits or explicit justified nonblocking deferrals.

Pure relocations can precede a behavior change when current behavior is meaningfully protected. Split independent deliverables when necessary, update the table and preserve dependencies. Do not merge unrelated work merely because it shares an audit ID, and do not split every finding into a separate plan or regression test.

## Release gates and exclusions

Qualification requires every material investigation finding and every assessed source/test file to have a delivered or justified explicit disposition. An included capability's broken invariant cannot be labelled nonblocking. Supported history must be reconstructible under the chosen policy; unsupported classes must be explicitly handled. Guidance/ADR routes and enforceable tooling must describe current truth, and canonical validation alone cannot establish unexercised behavior.

Excluded from this roadmap: Google identity and per-user ownership; hosted public/preprod admission and developer exclusion; Docker/Compose/IaC/VPS work; hosted migration/deploy orchestration; production-to-preprod replay transfer; multi-playthrough selection; cross-tab push; lawman, gunfight, additional vendors, independent bounty targets, poster circulation and new hunt completion. Their existing stories/dependencies remain in the cloud or deferred-feature records. Exclusion means no public deployment claim, not permission to erase a known boundary.
